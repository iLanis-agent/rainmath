🌧 RainMath

Rainwater harvesting math that holds up. Catchment, demand, tank sizing - numbers you can plan a system on.

What a storm gives you

One inch of rain on one square foot of roof is 0.623 gallons. Give it your roof and a storm; get the honest capture after losses.

What the garden wants

Gardens drink about an inch a week in season. Convert your beds to gallons and see how long a tank really lasts.

Dry-spell tank sizing

The tank's job is the dry spell, not the storm. Size it against your daily draw and the gap between rains.

First flush & fill plan

The first water off a dry roof carries the dust and droppings - divert it. Then see how many storms fill your tank.
